How it works

Connect. Instruct. Automate.

Co-Agent lives on your Mac and operates your business tools on your behalf — like having a virtual assistant that never sleeps.

Email & Follow-ups

Flags what matters, drafts replies in your voice, and follows up on time — even when you forget.

CRM & Pipeline

Updates contacts, logs interactions, and moves deals through your pipeline. Works with HubSpot, Salesforce, and more.

Calendar & Scheduling

Manages your calendar, preps you before meetings, and recaps after. Blocks focus time automatically.

Long-term Memory

Remembers your clients, preferences, and context across every conversation. Gets smarter the more you use it.

Documents & Reports

Creates proposals, reports, and client deliverables from conversation. Export to PDF with your branding.

Privacy First

Runs locally on your Mac. Your data never leaves your machine. No cloud storage, no third-party access.
